eOffice Activity Helper

This extension prevents idle-timeout logouts on eOffice by sending periodic, lightweight background heartbeats to /efile-api/date. It uses your existing session cookies, never accesses credentials or forms, and never reloads or navigates the page.









Tip: If Chrome or Edge puts background tabs to sleep to save RAM ("Memory Saver" / "Sleeping tabs"), add eoffice.wbsedcl.in to your browser's "Always keep these sites active" list under Settings → Performance.
